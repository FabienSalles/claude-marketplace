# French tech freelance market

Title taxonomy, rate bands and sourcing behaviour a profile and a CV should be aimed at
on the French market. Read when choosing a title or a positioning.

All figures are August 2026 relevés. Each carries a methodological caveat stated with
it; do not quote a number from this file without its caveat.

## 1. The title sets the price

On this market the job title, not the technology, sets the price. A community daily-rate
barometer, filtered on more than fifteen years of experience in the Paris region, gives:

| Title | €/day |
|---|---|
| Developer PHP/Symfony | 523 |
| Lead developer / tech lead | 655 |
| Technical consultant | 690 |
| AI/ML developer | 700 |
| Technical architect | 739 |

Same person, same engagement: a 216 €/day spread from the label alone.

**Caveat.** The barometer is fed by anonymous community contributions and its per-role
sample size is not published. Gaps between roles (523 vs 739) are large enough to
survive that noise. Gaps between adjacent experience bands inside one role are not:
never build an argument on those.

## 2. Volume confirms the same hierarchy

On a freelance mission board, missions returned per query:

| Query | Missions |
|---|---|
| architecte | 438 |
| tech lead | 215 |
| php | 55 |
| symfony | 18 |
| craft | 7 |

A profile titled on the technology appears in none of the 438 architect searches.

## 4. French beats English

Against the common assumption, French wins on this market. "Ingénieur IA" outweighs "AI
engineer" roughly ten to one; "ingénieur logiciel" beats "software engineer". The single
exception is "tech lead", an English term with no competitive French equivalent.

The most effective advertisers put both languages in one title. Do the same.

## 5. Titles that do not exist in France

- **"Software craftsman" / "artisan développeur"**: zero postings. The craft vocabulary
  does exist on the French market, but as a **qualifier inside the body** of a posting
  (TDD, clean code, hexagonal architecture, pair programming), never as a title. It is
  sold attached to a stack and a role.
- **"CTO à temps partagé"**: zero postings. The need may exist, but it does not travel
  through job boards or platform matching engines, so it makes a profile structurally
  invisible on those channels.
- **"Expert Symfony"**: the **query** works and returns many results, but no posting
  carries that **title**. Real titles observed: Senior Backend Engineer PHP/Symfony,
  Lead Développeur PHP Symfony, Tech Lead PHP, Développeur Symfony Senior. "Expert" is a
  level qualifier used inside agency postings, not a job title.

## 6. The rule: ROLE + STACK

Build the title as **role + stack, never stack alone**. The role captures the volume,
the stack captures the qualification.

Validated forms observed in real postings:

- Tech Lead PHP / Symfony
- Architecte technique PHP / Symfony
- Lead Developer PHP Symfony

For AI, attach it to an existing role rather than creating a pure AI title.

## 7. Spelling variants a sourcer will query

A sourcer's boolean will use these forms; the profile must contain them somewhere.

Développeur PHP · Developpeur PHP (unaccented, frequent) · Dev PHP · Développeur PHP
Symfony · Développeur PHP/Symfony · Développeur Back End PHP · Développeur Backend
PHP/Symfony · Senior Backend Developer Symfony · Backend Engineer PHP · Lead Developer
PHP Symfony · Tech Lead PHP · Ingénieur Symfony · Ingénieur Logiciel Symfony ·
Architecte Développeur Symfony

Canonical French sourcing pattern:

```
(Développeur OR Développeuse OR Developer OR Dev) AND (PHP OR Symfony)
```

## 8. Platforms: two opposite models

They require opposite strategies. Do not apply one family's playbook to the other.

**Algorithmic marketplaces, where the profile IS the product.** The dominant French one
publishes its matching field priority: 1. skills tags, 2. job title, 3. daily rate,
4. location, with a minimum 70% adequacy threshold. Two consequences: a perfect title
with poor skill tags does not surface; and the platform explicitly states that an
**excess** of keywords, like an artificially low rate, **reduces** selection chances.
Answer every opportunity including refusals, since that trains future matching. A second
board is a job board plus community rate barometer with no proprietary matching, where
visibility comes from applying.

**Selective staffing, where a human account manager presents two or three profiles.**
The qualification interview decides, not the profile page.

## 9. Channel reality

Against the "wait to be headhunted" assumption: the French executive employment
association's 2026 study (1150 companies, January-February 2026) finds the **published
job posting remains the number one channel**, used by 84% and cited as most effective by
54%, while direct approach fell to 63% and use of intermediaries to 42%. The first
stated recruiting difficulty is mismatch between applications received and the profile
sought (75%).

## 10. Market context

The French digital sector grew about 2% in 2025 with 4.3% forecast for 2026, but the
services/consultancy segment contracted 1.8% in 2025 while software publishers and
platforms grew 8.2%. Budget is shifting from staffing toward product, which favours
profiles that can build a product over profiles that fill a seat.

PHP itself remains a tension occupation on the national indicator. The problem is not
demand; it is the value ceiling of the label.

## 11. Direct versus intermediary

The barometer shows the rate gap between direct client and intermediary:

| Role | Direct client | Intermediary |
|---|---|---|
| Tech lead | 590 | 588 |
| Architect | 657 | 634 |
| PHP developer | 368 | 396 |

Essentially nil for tech lead, slightly favouring direct for architect, and **negative**
for PHP developer. The intermediary's margin is taken on the client, not deducted from
the contractor.

The lever is not the channel, it is the title. Choose the channel on mission quality and
relationship, not on a supposed rate gain.

## 12. French CV conventions

- The photo has never been mandatory and is decreasingly recommended, though a large
  share of French executives still include one.
- Age and date of birth are legally optional.
- The norm is one page below five years of experience, two beyond.

## 13. Positioning white space

Drawn from competitive positioning material.

| Saturated | Open |
|---|---|
| Full-service agency | Specialist in a specific vertical or problem |
| Affordable freelancer | Productised service with fixed scope |
| 10+ years of experience | Teaching or training delivered alongside the work |

**Warning that came with it:** a generic "AI-powered" positioning is the current
equivalent of an earlier buzzword wave. Avoid it unless AI is genuinely the
differentiator.
